About Us

Electro Future is an import and export business for electronics parts and gadgets. We source from trusted suppliers around the world and bring components, boards, and consumer devices to customers who need them.

What we offer

  • Electronic components, development boards and kits
  • Replacement parts such as TV motherboards
  • Gadgets and accessories
  • Direct supplier links on every product, so you can check specs yourself

How ordering works

  • Pick a product and tap Order on WhatsApp.
  • We confirm availability and price with you.
  • You pay by QR code on WhatsApp.
  • We ship within 3 to 7 business days.
